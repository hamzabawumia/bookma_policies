# Cancellation Policy

**BOOKMA** · Operated by Ican Institute · Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how appointments and accounts on **https://bookma1.com** can be cancelled.
BOOKMA has **no subscriptions**: nothing renews or is charged automatically.

## 1. Cancelling or rescheduling an appointment (clients)
- To cancel or reschedule, contact the business you booked, or email us. <!-- [CONFIRM: can clients cancel in the app?] -->
- Please give the business as much notice as you can, so the time can be offered to someone else.
- The platform fee is **not refunded** when you cancel.
- If you reschedule with the same business, your platform fee stays with the booking. Each booking can be rescheduled **up to two times** on the same platform fee. After two reschedules, a new booking and a new platform fee are needed.
- If you miss an appointment without telling the business, the platform fee isn't refunded.

## 2. If the business cancels
If the business cancels or can't see you at the booked time, the booking is marked for rescheduling and a new time is
agreed with you. Your platform fee stays with the booking. This counts towards the two reschedules allowed per booking.

## 3. Closing your account
- **Clients** can ask us to close their account at any time by emailing us.
- **Businesses** can close their profile by emailing us. Please let clients with upcoming appointments know first.
- Payment records are kept for as long as the law requires, as explained in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
