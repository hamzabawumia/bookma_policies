# Privacy Policy

**BOOKMA** · Operated by Ican Institute · Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data BOOKMA collects on **https://bookma1.com** and how we use and protect it.
We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

## 1. What we collect
**Clients**
- **Account details:** name, email address, phone number and password (stored encrypted).
- **Bookings:** the business you booked, and the date and time of each appointment.

**Businesses**
- Business or professional name, contact details, location, profile details, opening hours and appointment length.

**Everyone**
- **Payment records:** for each payment, the amount, currency, date, status and payment reference. **We do not
  receive or store card numbers, mobile money PINs or bank details.** Our payment gateways handle those.
- **Technical data:** standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) and a login session cookie.

## 2. How we use it
- To create and run accounts, and to show businesses and their free times to clients.
- To make, confirm and manage bookings, and to send appointment reminders.
- To process platform fees.
- To answer your questions and keep the platform secure.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

## 3. Who can see your data
- **The business you book** sees your name, contact details and booking, so it knows who is coming.
- **Clients** see a business's public profile, location and free times.
- **BOOKMA staff**, only when needed to run the platform or help you.

## 4. Who we share data with
- **Payment gateways** such as Paystack, to process payments. Their privacy policies apply to the details you enter on
  their checkout.
- **Messaging services** such as email, SMS or WhatsApp, to send booking confirmations and reminders.
- **Our hosting provider**, which stores the platform's data on our behalf.
- Authorities, only when the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it
- Account and booking data are kept while the account is active.
- Payment records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires.
- You can ask us to delete your account.

## 6. Security
Passwords and payment keys are stored encrypted, and the platform uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we
take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## 7. Your rights
You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address
below. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
