# Refund Policy

**BOOKMA** · Operated by Ican Institute · Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the platform fee paid on **https://bookma1.com**.

## 1. No refunds
**BOOKMA does not offer refunds. The platform fee is final once paid.**

Clients pay a platform fee online, shown in Ghana Cedis (GHS) before paying, to confirm an appointment. Please check
the business, date and time on the payment page before you pay.

## 2. What the platform fee covers
The platform fee covers the use of BOOKMA to find a business, book and confirm your time slot, and receive reminders.
It is charged by BOOKMA independently of the business's own charges.

## 3. The business's own charges
The business's charges for its service (for example a haircut, consultation or repair) are separate from the platform
fee and are paid to the business at its premises. Any question about those charges is between you and the business.

## 4. Rescheduling
Your platform fee stays with your booking when it is rescheduled. This applies when you reschedule, when your time is
taken while you're paying, or when the business can't see you at the booked time. It is a rebooking, not a refund.

A booking can be rescheduled **up to two times** on the same platform fee. After two reschedules, a new booking
and a new platform fee are needed. See our [Cancellation Policy](cancellation.md).

## 5. Payment problems
If you've paid but your booking isn't showing as confirmed, email **icaninstitute.org@gmail.com** with your name, the
phone number on your account, and the **payment reference** from your receipt. We'll confirm the booking or agree a new
time with the business (this doesn't count as one of your two reschedules). We reply within 3 working days.
