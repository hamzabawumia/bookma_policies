## 1. What we provide
BOOKMA is an online appointment booking system. Through it:
- **businesses** (companies, professionals, artisans and individuals) can create a profile, set their opening hours and
  appointment length, get an Appointments Poster, and see who has booked;
- **clients** can find a business, choose a free date and time, and book an appointment.

## 2. Our role and the business's role
- BOOKMA provides the booking technology. The services themselves (a haircut, a legal consultation, a car repair and

- Businesses must give accurate details about who they are, where they are and the hours they are available. We may
  remove a profile that is false, misleading or offers unlawful services.

## 3. Accounts
- Businesses and clients need an account. Please give accurate details, including your name and phone number.

- A parent or guardian should book on behalf of anyone under 18.

## 4. Prices and payment
- Creating a business account and listing your hours on BOOKMA is **free**.
- Clients pay a **platform fee** to BOOKMA when they confirm an appointment. The fee is shown in **Ghana Cedis (GHS)**
  before you pay. It confirms and holds your time slot.
- The platform fee is charged **independently of the business's own charges**. The business's charges for its service
  are separate and are paid to the business at its premises.
- **We do not offer refunds. The platform fee is final once paid.** See our [Refund Policy](refund.md).
- Payments are processed securely by **Paystack and other payment gateways**, by mobile money or card. We never see or

## 5. Bookings
- A time is held for you while you pay. If it's taken before your payment completes, BOOKMA marks the booking for
  rescheduling and a new time is agreed with the business.
- When a booking is rescheduled, the platform fee stays with it. A booking can be rescheduled **up to two times** on the same platform fee. After two reschedules, a new booking and a new platform fee are needed.
- Please arrive on time. For cancelling or rescheduling, see our [Cancellation Policy](cancellation.md).
